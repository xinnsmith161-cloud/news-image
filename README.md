A newspaper front page summing up Friday, 20 November 2026, built for the
[Sum up 20 November's world news in one image bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120)
on make.fun. The program (`main.ts`, Node 22 standard library only) copies
`entry.png` — a broadsheet front page drawn with PIL — to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.

## Stories covered

- **Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — first-ever three-way talks end
  with a "peace declaration", but no breakthrough on Ukraine.
- **Kyiv under fire** — Russia fired 100+ missiles and 400 drones overnight;
  three Kyiv bridges hit, power cut across the capital, Poland scrambled jets.
- **Court: Myanmar committed genocide** — the UN's top court (ICJ) finds
  Myanmar guilty of genocide against the Rohingya, 9–6 vote.
- **Anthropic's $2 trillion debut** — the first purely AI company to list
  publicly; shares soared 34% on its Wall Street debut, the biggest IPO ever.
- **Also today** — US and Venezuela restore military ties; UK–EU summit agrees
  a youth mobility scheme; Mexico's president flies economy to APEC and goes
  viral in China; Trump pardons baseball legend Pete Rose.
